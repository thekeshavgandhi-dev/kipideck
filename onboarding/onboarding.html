Welcome to Kipideck

Kipideck saves things you point it at — a page, a link, an image, or text you selected — and keeps them searchable on this device. Before it saves anything on its own, here is exactly what that means.

🔒 Nothing is captured automatically until you choose below. Explicit saves — right-click → Save to Kipi, the toolbar popup, or CtrlShiftK — always work.

What Kipideck can save

  • 📄
    The page you save — its title, URL, domain, and the site's own preview image and description. Only when you save that page.
  • 🔎
    Up to 20,000 characters of that page's visible text, so you can search inside articles later instead of just by title. Only for pages you save. Never for pages you merely visit.
  • ✂️
    Text you select on a page, saved as a quote with a link back to it. Only if “Auto-save my selections” is on below.
  • 🔗
    A link or image you right-click — the URL, and the page it was on. Only on that right-click.
  • 🏷️
    Your own notes and tags, and the deck you file things into. Only what you type or choose.
  • 🎨
    The site's favicon, fetched from the site itself and cached on your device so the library looks like a library. Only for domains you have saved. No third-party icon service is ever asked.

What Kipideck never does

  • 🚫
    No browsing history. It only ever sees the page you act on.
  • 🚫
    No keystrokes, no form contents, no passwords, and nothing from your other tabs.
  • 🚫
    No analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels, no crash reporting, no third-party requests of any kind.
  • 🚫
    No Kipideck server. There isn't one — so there is nothing we could read, sell, or lose in a breach.

Where your data lives

  • 💾
    On this device, in this browser profile (IndexedDB). Your library works fully offline, and deleting the extension deletes it.
  • ☁️
    Optionally, in your own Google Drive — a hidden “app data” folder only Kipideck can read, using your own OAuth client. Off until you set it up in Library → Settings → Sync.
  • 📦
    Exportable any time as JSON from the Library, and importable back — merges with what you already have instead of replacing it.

Your decks, and the keys

Saved items are filed automatically. These are the built-in decks — rename them or add your own from the Library.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    CtrlShiftK Save the page you're reading
    CtrlShiftL Open your library
    SpaceK Quick-save — if you left the toggle on above, and never on sites where those keys already do something
    Ctrl↵ Finish here, right now

    On macOS use ⌘ instead of Ctrl.

    Choosing either button turns Kipideck on. Nothing leaves your device.

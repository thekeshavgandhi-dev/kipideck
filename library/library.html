Sync across devices & browsers

Kipideck can sync your decks through your own private Google Drive "app data" folder — invisible in your normal Drive, readable only by Kipideck. No account or server of ours involved.

One-time setup by whoever installs Kipideck — see docs/GOOGLE_SYNC_SETUP.md or the illustrated 5-minute guide for the Google Cloud Console steps. Credentials stay in this browser profile; Kipideck has no server to send them to.
